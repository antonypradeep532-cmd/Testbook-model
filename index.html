GOOD QUESTIONS, HONEST ANSWERS

Before you get started.

Which examinations can I prepare for here?+

TNPSC_TAMILQUIZ brings together preparation paths for TNPSC, SSC, Railway and Banking examinations, with exam-focused practice and learning resources.

Are the sample mock tests free?+

Some sample tests are marked free and can be attempted directly. Other test types are represented as previews in this demonstration frontend.

Can I switch the interface to Tamil?+

Use the language switch in the top navigation to change supported interface labels. Study content includes bilingual examples where available.

Are the job listings official?+

No. Job cards are clearly labelled sample data and link only to official organization homepages. Always confirm dates and eligibility in the official notification.

Will my progress be saved?+

Demo preferences, quiz history, bookmarks and planner tasks are saved in this browser using local storage.
